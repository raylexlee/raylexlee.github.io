Macros in WoW Forever can seem confusing, but they're one of the easiest ways to clean up your action bars and play more smoothly.

We go over how macros work, piece by piece, so you can write your own mouseover, modifier and utility macros with confidence.

Check The Guide ➡️: https://youtu.be/Hrh8107iHsk

📝 WoW Forever EZ Macros 📝

Mod Spell
#showtooltip
/startattack
/cast [nomod:shift] spell; spell

Mouseover Heal Downranks
#showtooltip
/target [@mouseover]
/cast [nomod] Healing Touch(Rank 2); [mod:shift] Healing Touch(Rank 1); [mod:ctrl] Healing Touch(Rank 3)

Multibox Heal Macro
#showtooltip
/cast [mod:shift, @player] Regrowth; [@Lorutank] Regrowth

Focus Util Macro
#showtooltip Kick
/cast [@focus,exists,nodead] Kick; Kick

Focus Moon Util Macro
#showtooltip Polymorph
/focus
/tm [@focus] 5
/cast [@focus,exists,nodead] Polymorph; Polymorph
/s i am polying the moon

Mouseover Heal Macro:
#showtooltip Flash Heal
/cast [@mouseover,help,nodead][] Flash Heal

Pet Attacks Macro
#showtooltip
/petattack
/Serpent Sting

Pet Control Macro
/petpassive
/petfollow

Ability Combine Macro
#showtooltip
/startattack
/cast Rend
/cast Overpower

Multibox Buffs Macro
#showtooltip
/cast [btn:2, @Loruhots] Thorns; [@Lorutank] Thorns

2x Gather Macro
#showtooltip
/castsequence !Find Herbs, !Find Minerals
